# ADR-0060: Task material and proportionate instructions

Status: Accepted for compatibility design.

## Context

Repeated entry material can increase reading cost, but output volume alone
does not prove Token savings or a quality improvement.

## Decision

Keep default stage material and all current safety/authority checks. Add explicit `--material task` to separate complete relevant actor inventory records and non-current gate references from mandatory source bodies. Unknown shapes/relations and independent full-source requirements retain full material. Scope, approval, risk, policy and current evidence are not replaced by a machine-only summary. See the exact supported categories in context routing.

Retain caller-attested availability as an independent option, with no
persisted session cache or provider-loading claim. Bind every representation
to full-source and entry digests. This read-only representation cannot
satisfy a lifecycle gate or authorize mutation.

Clarify the distribution operating instructions: route optional project records by task, reuse canonical evidence rather than duplicate narratives, use the generated handoff once, and make parallel plan/prepare conditional on dispatch. Do not remove required tests, independent identities, bootstrap or human approval. Do not modify frozen experiment prompts or installed managed Skills to manufacture a cheaper result.

## Verification and limits

Test installed default/task paths, shape/requirement fallbacks, evidence categories, actor relations, freshness, source failures and reuse composition. Record both body and serialized bytes on synthetic fixtures. Full fixed-candidate verification and distinct QA are required; no model-efficiency or universal role-reduction claim follows.

Rollback is removal of the opt-in mode and restoration of the prior distribution wording. No canonical data migration or profile change is introduced.
