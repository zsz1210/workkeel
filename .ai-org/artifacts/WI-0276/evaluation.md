an unknown journal result. It was not replayed, reconciled into success or used

Limits: one ordered pair per scenario; synthetic small tasks; cache/order and
some early verification resource overlap; no representative speed/cost claim.
Controlled bytes exclude source reads and hidden host context. Preflight timing
does not measure all material preparation or human coordination. Historical
experiments remain immutable. Full local test evidence and independent QA are
separate gates; this evaluation is not their substitute.

Raw analysis, CSV, method versions, per-turn metrics, original operations and
source outputs are retained in the user-facing context-delivery report bundle.
No new large study is required to accept this bounded implementation.
